# First build ledger (Thursday's slice)

Started Thu Nov 12, 10:45 AM Pacific on GameStation (builder), branch `Gamestation/first-build`, from `main`.
Method from [`INITIAL_PROMPT.md`](INITIAL_PROMPT.md): a lead that plans and keeps this file, one builder subagent per
packet, one checker subagent per packet that never sees the builder's report. Only a checker's pass makes `verified`.
Where two packets were small and touched the same Blueprints, one builder built both and one checker checked both;
the Model column says "with X". B2 and E1 were planned for Sonnet and went to Opus because they were paired with an
Opus packet, not because of a failed check. No packet failed its check twice; every fail became a fix packet.

**Scope as set in the morning (Team Owner, Nov 12; changed that afternoon, see the next note but one):** jam-sized, not the large city. This run builds today's checkpoint from
[`ROADMAP.md`](ROADMAP.md): player, fists, goons, Rewind and the Freeze Frame boss, in grey boxes, playable from
start to finish in one small level (a cafe, one street, one plaza). No anime look (undecided, on its own branch),
no art, no sound, no cut scenes (markers only). Splice, Stunt Double and the Director are Friday's, not in this run.

**Everything here is untested by a person.** Nothing reaches `playtest` or `main` until someone has played it.

**Design change, Nov 12 afternoon (Team Owner):** Freeze Frame is the first power, handed over by the driver in the
cafe; Rewind moves to a later boss (its work here is kept, switched off); the cel-shaded anime look is in. Packets
G1 and A3 below carry that.

**Keys (polled in Tick, keyboard and gamepad):** move and look = the template's. Attack = left mouse / gamepad X.
Rewind = Q / left shoulder (parked). Freeze Frame = E / right shoulder. Restart on an end screen = R / gamepad A.

**The level, `Lvl_JumpCut`** (real scale, all grey boxes): the cafe (a 12 x 10 m room with a crashed-car box and a
driver box) opens onto a street about 60 m long with one group of goons and a checkpoint at its end, which leads
into a walled plaza about 30 x 30 m. A gate closes behind the player and the plaza boss wakes up. Beating
him opens the far gate, and the win screen shows a few steps beyond it. (As first planned he handed over Freeze
Frame; since G1 the driver in the cafe does.)

## Notes for checkers (from the design note, [`FIRST_BUILD_DESIGN.md`](FIRST_BUILD_DESIGN.md))
- `PlayerPunch` only hits what is in front: face the player at the enemy first (`PlayerDebugTeleport` takes a yaw).
- A beaten enemy is destroyed 0.6 s later, not at once, so the knockback can be seen.
- Measure Rewind only after `IsRewinding` is false again. Hits are ignored during the slide and for 0.5 s after.
- Gates report open at once but the mesh takes about half a second to slide: capture after that.
- While Rewind is parked (`HasRewind` false, since G1), health 0 is lost at once. With Rewind on there is a 1.5 s
  grace window at 0 health; that was the design, not a bug.
- "The player can / can't move" can't be proven without keys. Check the variables; a person plays the rest.
- The win marker is only guarded by the exit gate: the run-through (E2) must go through the boss, not teleport past him.
- There must be exactly one live `BP_Player_C`, placed in the level with auto-possess, and the game mode must keep
  the template's player controller.

## What a person must still play (from the run-through, in play order)
1. **Title:** press R (or gamepad A). The game starts on the first press and the health bar appears. With A, does the character hop?
2. **Cafe:** WASD and mouse work now and were dead on the title. Walk to the cyan ball by the blue driver box: it vanishes and a cyan E icon appears. Is it clear what you got and from whom?
3. **Head for the door without visiting the driver:** you still get the power at the door. Is that what we want?
4. **Street, left mouse / gamepad X on the goons:** three clicks give the combo with the big third hit. Do hits feel like hits? Is one combo for all three too easy by hand?
5. **E / right shoulder:** enemies stop, cyan border, the bar drains for 10 s, the icon dims for 20 s. Do beaten goons that stay standing read as beaten?
6. **Q / left shoulder anywhere:** nothing happens (Rewind is parked).
7. **Checkpoint post:** the green cube shows. Do you notice it?
8. **Plaza entry:** the gate drops behind you without touching you and the boss comes. Try turning straight back out.
9. **Boss:** a red ball warns of each punch; he never freezes you. Beat him once with E and once without.
10. **Lose on purpose (to the goons, then to the boss):** "CUT!" and the world holds still. R or A restarts at the right place with full health.
11. **Exit gate and the walk beyond:** "THAT'S A WRAP".
12. **Win screen, R or A:** back to the title with everything reset. Also hold R for a second: is the title skipped?

## For the team to tune (checker's numbers at defaults; nothing was retuned)
- A scripted run takes 25 to 27 s. The pitch wants 3 to 5 minutes.
- Goons bunch into the punch arc: one three-hit combo beat all three in 1.0 s with no damage taken.
- The boss falls to 6.3 s of plain punching (he is never interrupted); the player loses 30 health.
- Freeze Frame lasts 10 s, longer than either fight, so it makes both trivial; then its 20 s cooldown means it isn't back for the boss on a fast run.
- Enemies beaten during a Freeze Frame stay standing until it ends (seen in captures). That they block the lane is
  inferred from a slower walk to the checkpoint (5.4 s against 3.8 s), not measured.
- 5 s of empty walking from the cafe door to the goons. No boss health display. The key prompts are bare symbols ("R / (A)").
